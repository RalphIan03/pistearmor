Ballistic Architecture

Anatomy of Total Protection

Explore the 6 critical engineering pillars integrated into every PISTE vehicle to achieve 360-degree protection without secondary spalling or seam vulnerability.

1

Ballistic Glass with Spall Shield

Multi-layer optical float glass laminated with polycarbonates. Prevents glass shards from flying inward into occupants during sustained bullet impacts.

2

Labyrinth Perimeter Door Overlaps

Patented interlocking ballistic steel plates enclosing all door pillars and window seams, eliminating bullet penetration through body gaps.

3

500+ HBW Armored Cabin Capsule

Precision laser-cut ballistic steel cell encapsulating entire passenger compartment (roof, firewall, pillars, quarter panels, and rear bulkhead).

4

Underbody Blast Mitigation Shield

Multi-layer armored floor plating engineered to absorb and deflect ground detonation shockwaves from 2x simultaneous DM51 / HG85 hand grenades.

5

Run-Flat Beadlock Wheel Systems

Solid lightweight polymer rings clamping tire bead directly to rim, maintaining steering control and 50 km/h escape speed under zero tire pressure.

6

Protected ECU & Dual Armored Batteries

Engine control modules, auxiliary batteries, radiator firewall, and self-sealing fuel tank are shielded to guarantee vehicle driveability during ambush.

Precision Methodology

5-Stage Engineering Protocol

Every armored conversion follows our strict aerospace-grade protocol to ensure zero structural weaknesses and uncompromised safety.

01

Threat Analysis & 3D CAD

Comprehensive risk profiling followed by 3D optical scanning of the OEM vehicle chassis to design custom interlocking armor plates.

02

Complete Disassembly

Vehicle is meticulously stripped to its bare structural frame. Interior trim, electrical harnesses, and delicate sensors are safely cataloged.

03

Armor Capsule Fabrication

Certified ballistic steel and aramid composite shells are welded with continuous overlap protection to prevent angular trajectory penetrations.

04

Chassis & Dynamic Refit

Heavy-duty suspension, upsized multi-piston brakes, reinforced door hinges, run-flat inserts, and emergency systems are calibrated.

05

QA, Certification & Delivery

Laser alignment, water deluge testing, acoustic verification, and official VPAM / CEN ballistic certification dossier compilation.
